      How the images are made

      1. The NASA/IPAC Infrared Science Archive (IRSA) lists every SPHEREx spectral image that covers the position. No data release is named in the query, so new images appear here as they are published.
      2. A small cutout around the position is taken from each image. The zodiacal light model that ships with the image and the remaining background are subtracted, and broken pixels are left out.
      3. Images taken within the same visit are aligned on a north-up grid of 3″ pixels and combined with a median, one detector at a time, so every visit covers the same wavelengths.
      4. Each image sees the target at its own wavelength. Measuring the target in every image and plotting brightness against that wavelength gives the spectrum.

      Every number on this page is either measured from the SPHEREx images or taken from a catalogue, and says which.
